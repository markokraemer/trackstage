# learn.sessionboard.com — verbatim video transcripts

Every `/videos/*` page on Sessionboard's help centre ships a full timestamped transcript inside a
`<details>Video transcript</details>` block. These were captured during the 2026-08-11 crawl and
are reproduced verbatim (only whitespace normalised) so the narration layer of the ingestion is
durable and independently checkable against
[`docs/reference/sessionboard-product-map.md`](../../reference/sessionboard-product-map.md).

Three videos ship no transcript — **Portals (Pro)** and **AI evaluations** (YouTube embeds) and
**Importing data** (Loom embed); see `README.md`.
